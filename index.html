2026 ISHLT fellowship project

Use EVLP as a window for targeted treatment.

The fellowship project, “Click Chemistry for Targeted mRNA Delivery During Ex Vivo Lung Perfusion,” develops a strategy intended to improve donor-lung quality by guiding mRNA-carrying lipid nanoparticles during organ support.

1Maintain and evaluate a donor lung during ex vivo lung perfusion.
2Use click chemistry to support selective molecular targeting.
3Assess targeted mRNA delivery in an organ-level therapeutic setting.

Professional profile & education

Clinical questions.
Multidisciplinary preparation.

Training in medicine, chemistry, East Asian studies, computational chemistry, regenerative medicine and thoracic research provides a broad foundation for translational lung-transplantation science.

Daniele is an MD, PhD physician-scientist whose career connects clinical surgery with experimental organ research.

Her current postdoctoral appointment at University Health Network focuses on donor-lung engineering. Her doctoral work examined decellularised lung extracellular-matrix hydrogel in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis models, and her earlier research at Massachusetts General Hospital addressed organ engineering and regeneration.

She completed medical training in Brazil, including clinical experiences in thoracic transplantation, trauma surgery and advanced general surgery in Canada and the United States.
