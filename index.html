About

Clinical questions.
Engineering solutions.

A multidisciplinary path connecting medical insight with chemistry, biomaterials and transplantation science.

Daniele Evangelista is a physician-scientist working at the intersection of lung transplantation, molecular delivery, tissue engineering and regenerative medicine.

As a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Toronto General Hospital within University Health Network, Daniele investigates therapies that can be applied during ex vivo lung perfusion—when a donor lung is supported and assessed outside the body—to create a controlled window for intervention before transplantation.

Medical training at the University of Campinas and doctoral research at the University of São Paulo provide the clinical and mechanistic foundation for this work. The PhD program examined decellularized lung extracellular-matrix hydrogels in models of idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis.

Current fellowship project

Intervening before the transplant begins.

The 2026 ISHLT Research Fellowship supports a precision-delivery strategy designed for use during ex vivo lung perfusion.

ISHLT Research Fellowship · 2026

Click Chemistry for Targeted mRNA Delivery During Ex Vivo Lung Perfusion

A novel strategy to improve donor lung quality.

The project uses click chemistry to guide mRNA-carrying lipid nanoparticles toward selected cells during EVLP. The approach is designed to support precise treatment where it is needed most, with the potential to enhance lung repair, reduce early graft dysfunction and increase the number of lungs suitable for transplantation.
