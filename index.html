Professional profile

Clinical questions.
Translational solutions.

Her training connects chemistry, medicine, organ engineering, regenerative science and lung transplantation.

Daniele is a physician-scientist whose work is shaped by both clinical training and experimental organ research.

At the Keshavjee Lab, her current project focuses on the localization of therapeutic molecules in donor lungs during ex vivo lung perfusion. The work uses a transplant-relevant platform to study where treatments travel and how precisely they reach intended cells before implantation.

Her doctoral research at the University of São Paulo examined decellularized lung extracellular-matrix hydrogel in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis models, including inflammatory, oxidative, remodelling and fibrotic pathways.

Organ intervention

Turn preservation time into treatment time.

Ex vivo lung perfusion maintains a donor lung outside the body in a controlled, functioning state, creating a window for physiological assessment and direct intervention before implantation.

1Observe lung function under controlled perfusion and ventilation.
2Apply treatment directly to the donor organ before transplantation.
3Measure physiological and molecular response during support.

Click Chemistry for Targeted mRNA Delivery During Ex Vivo Lung Perfusion

A novel strategy to improve donor lung quality.

The project develops a targeted approach for delivering mRNA-carrying lipid nanoparticles during EVLP. Click chemistry is used to guide the carrier toward selected cells while the donor lung is supported outside the body.
